FAQ

Good to know

Which engines and tools do you use?

Unity (C#), Roblox Studio (Lua) and Unreal Engine for games, and Blender for 3D characters, props and level kits.

Do I own the game and the files?

Yes. After the order is complete you get the project and source files, with commercial rights.

Can you work from my idea or sketch?

Yes. Send your idea, sketches or games you like, and I will turn them into a clear plan before building.

How long does a game take?

Small prototypes take about 1–2 weeks. Bigger games are split into milestones, each with its own fixed quote.
