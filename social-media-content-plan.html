Plan 30 days of social media content in one afternoon

Posting consistently is the secret to growing on Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest. Here is a simple monthly plan you can follow — plus a printable planner.

The weekly rhythm

  • Monday — a tip or how-to (gets saves).
  • Tuesday — product spotlight.
  • Wednesday — behind the scenes.
  • Thursday — customer review.
  • Friday — offer or new arrival.
  • Weekend — a poll, question or quote.

10 post ideas to start

  1. Introduce yourself and your brand story.
  2. Share your best-selling product.
  3. Show how your product is made or packed.
  4. Answer a common question.
  5. Before & after transformation.
  6. Myth vs fact in your industry.
  7. A happy customer photo.
  8. Three ways to use your product.
  9. What's coming soon.
  10. A thank-you to your community.
Batch itCreate a whole week of posts in one sitting, then schedule them. It saves hours and keeps your feed consistent.
