Launch a Shopify store that looks professional from day one

A clean theme, clear banners and great product pages turn visitors into buyers. Here is what a ready-to-sell Shopify store needs — and how I can set it up for you.

What every new store needs

  1. A fast, mobile-friendly theme styled in your brand colours and fonts.
  2. A homepage banner with one clear message and one button.
  3. Product pages with 4–6 photos, benefit-led titles and short bullet points.
  4. Collections such as New In, Best Sellers and Sale.
  5. Trust pages — About, Contact, FAQ, Shipping and Refund policies.
  6. Checkout setup — payments, shipping rates and a test order.
Did you know?Most store visitors shop on their phone. Always check your homepage and product pages on mobile before launch.

Banners & product images that sell

Your banner is your shop window. Keep it simple: a lifestyle photo, a short headline (5–7 words) and a button like "Shop the collection". For product images use a light background for the main photo, then add a lifestyle photo, a close-up and one showing size.

Writing product titles

Use this formula: Brand + Product + Key feature + Size/Colour. For example: "Aseel Home Ceramic Vase – Matte Beige, 25 cm".
