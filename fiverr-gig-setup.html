Gig title formula

Start with "I will" + the result + who it's for. For example: "I will set up a professional Shopify store for your fashion brand."

Three packages

  • Basic — the smallest useful result.
  • Standard — your most popular option; make it the obvious best value.
  • Premium — the complete solution with extras and faster delivery.

Thumbnails that get clicks

  1. Bright background and a large, short title.
  2. Show real examples of the result.
  3. Keep colours consistent across all your gigs.
Tags matterUse five tags buyers actually type, mixing broad and specific phrases.
