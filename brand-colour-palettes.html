5 palettes to start with

  • Earthy & Calm — #3F4E3A, #7C8B6B, #D8CBB5, #F3EDE3, #B6865C. Great for home and wellness brands.
  • Bold & Modern — #1C1B3A, #5B3DF5, #FF6B8B, #FFC857, #F5F5F7. For tech, events and youthful brands.
  • Soft Pastel — #F7D6E0, #F2B5D4, #7BDFF2, #B2F7EF, #EFF7F6. For kids, beauty and gifting.
  • Luxury Gold — #111111, #2C2C2C, #C9A46A, #E8D5B0, #FAF7F2. For jewellery and premium services.
  • Fresh & Clean — #0B6E4F, #08A045, #6BBF59, #DDB771, #FFFFFF. For food, organic and outdoor brands.

How to choose your palette

  1. Write three words that describe your brand (e.g. calm, natural, handmade).
  2. Pick the palette that matches those words — not just your favourite colour.
  3. Test it: design one Instagram post and one product label. If both look good, you've found it.

Where to use your colours

Add the HEX codes to Canva's Brand Kit, your Shopify theme settings and your packaging. Consistency is what makes people remember you.
