About

Hi, I'm Pakiza

I'm a designer and virtual assistant based in Lahore, working remotely with small businesses around the world. My background is in fine arts and computer applications, and I've spent the last few years designing with Canva, Adobe and CorelDRAW.

I love the details — the right shade of beige, a font pairing that just works, a spreadsheet that finally makes sense. My goal is simple: make your brand look as good as the work you put into it, and give you back a few hours each week.
