5 rules for posts that stand out

  1. Stick to 2–3 brand colours — consistency builds recognition.
  2. Use bold, readable fonts — maximum two per design.
  3. Leave white space — let your message breathe.
  4. One message per post — don't crowd the design.
  5. Add a clear call to action — "Shop now", "DM us" or "Link in bio".

Post types every business needs

  • New arrivals & launches to create excitement.
  • Sale posts with a clear offer and deadline.
  • Tips & how-tos that people save and share.
  • Reviews that build trust.
  • Behind the scenes that show the people behind the brand.
Quick tipPlan your feed in rows of three. Alternating a photo, a graphic and a quote keeps it looking balanced.

Templates vs custom designs

Templates are a great start, but custom designs in your own colours and fonts make your brand unforgettable. I design post sets for small businesses — sale posts, launches, tips and reviews — and send editable Canva links so you can reuse them.
