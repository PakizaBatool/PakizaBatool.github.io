Build an aesthetic brand kit that makes your business look established

Your logo, colours and fonts are the first things customers notice. Here is how to create a simple brand kit that keeps everything consistent — and how I can make one for you in 48 hours.

What is a brand kit?

A brand kit is a one-page guide that shows exactly how your business looks: your logo, colour palette, fonts and a few example designs. Once you have it, every Instagram post, product label and website banner looks like it belongs to the same brand.

The 4 essentials

  1. Logo set — a main logo, a simple icon or monogram, and a one-colour version for small spaces.
  2. Colour palette — five colours: a dark for text, an accent for buttons, a mid tone, a light tone and a background.
  3. Fonts — one font for headings and one for body text. Two is enough; three is the maximum.
  4. Brand board — everything on one page so you (or anyone helping you) can follow it.
Quick tipUse the 60-30-10 rule: 60% background colour, 30% secondary colour and 10% accent. It keeps designs calm and professional.

How to choose colours that suit your business

Think about how you want customers to feel. Warm beiges and clay tones feel handmade and cosy; sage greens feel calm and natural; blush pinks feel soft and feminine; navy and gold feel premium. Pick one direction and stay with it everywhere.

Common mistakes

  • Using a different colour or font in every post.
  • A detailed logo that becomes unreadable on a phone screen.
  • Copying a big brand's colours exactly — customers notice.
