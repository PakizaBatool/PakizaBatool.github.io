Use Pinterest to bring free, steady traffic to your business

Pinterest is a search engine, not just a social app. The right pins keep sending visitors to your shop for months. Here is how to start.

Why Pinterest works

People come to Pinterest to plan and buy — home ideas, gifts, recipes, outfits and business tips. A good pin can appear in search results for months, unlike an Instagram post that disappears in a day.

What makes a pin get clicks

  1. Vertical size — 1000 × 1500 px.
  2. A clear headline on the image with your keyword.
  3. Aesthetic, on-brand design — soft colours and mockups perform well.
  4. A keyword-rich title and description.
  5. A link to a helpful page, not just your homepage.
Consistency wins1–2 fresh pins a day for 30 days does more than 20 pins in one day.

Set up your account right

  • Switch to a free business account.
  • Claim your website so your name appears on every pin.
  • Create 4–5 boards with keyword names.
